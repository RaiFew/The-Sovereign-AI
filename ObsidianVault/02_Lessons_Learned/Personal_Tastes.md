# Personal Tastes

## Content Generation Guidelines
- Tone: Objective yet engaging, skeptical of hype.
- Style: Direct, bullet-heavy, concise.
- Formatting: Use Markdown headers effectively, prefer tables for comparisons.
- Thai Translation constraints: Use formal but accessible Thai. Avoid overly academic terms unless necessary.

## Topic Preferences
- **Manga**: Focus on pacing, character development, and artwork consistency. Avoid generic synopsis.
- **Coding**: Prioritize clean architecture, testability, and edge cases. Prefer Python/TypeScript.
- **Trading**: Focus on risk management over expected returns. Require backtest references if applicable.
